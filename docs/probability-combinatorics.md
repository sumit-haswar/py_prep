addition rule for probability

P(cube of any color) =       13 (set of cubes)  / 29 (all possibilities)     =     13/29
P(yellow object)  = 12 / 29
P(yellow cube) = 5/29

P(yellow OR cube) =    12 (no. of yellow)  + [13 - 5]   / 29     = 20/29
                                =  12/29 + 13/29 - 5/29
                                = P(Y) + P(Cube) - P(yellow & cube)
